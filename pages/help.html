Help Center & Academic FAQ

Find immediate answers to questions on submissions, attendance records, and grading policies.

Contact Academic Help Desk
How do I submit assignments and what file formats are accepted?
Navigate to the Assignments tab from the sidebar. Select your pending coursework item and click "Submit Deliverable". You can drag and drop your file into the upload zone or choose a file from your hard drive. Accepted formats include PDF documents, compressed ZIP folders, SQL schemas, and Jupyter Notebooks (.ipynb). Once submitted, a confirmation receipt will be generated and faculty will be alerted.
How does attendance tracking work for Live Classrooms?
When you click "Join Live Classroom" during a scheduled lecture, Lernovique registers your attendance token with the university registrar server. Attending at least 75% of the live session automatically satisfies the statutory academic attendance threshold for mid-term eligibility.
How is Semester GPA (SGPA) and Cumulative GPA (CGPA) calculated?
Lernovique implements the standard UGC 10-point scale. SGPA is calculated by multiplying credit weighting of each course with grade point earned, divided by total registered credits. CGPA represents the cumulative weighted average across all completed semesters.
Can I switch between Student and Faculty viewpoints for demonstration?
Yes! For evaluation of this interactive prototype, you can switch roles anytime using the "Switch to Faculty" link in the sidebar or by logging in with either demo account: student@edu.com / 1234 or faculty@edu.com / 1234.
Privacy Policy & Data Protection
Lernovique is dedicated to safeguarding student and faculty data privacy. All academic records, assignments, and grades are secured using end-to-end encryption protocols in compliance with institutional guidelines and educational data confidentiality regulations. We do not sell or share student learning information with third-party advertising services.
Academic Integrity & Honor Code
All students enrolled on Lernovique agree to abide by the university Honor Code. Submitted assignments, project reports, and assessments must reflect original work. Automated plagiarism analysis and submission version timestamps are logged to preserve academic honesty, fairness, and professional integrity across all university departments.
